# Agrijuune Privacy and Demo-Data Notice

**Status:** Educational prototype; public enquiry collection is disabled
**Last updated:** 25 November 2026

## Project purpose

Agrijuune is being presented as a school-project prototype. The public
website demonstrates proposed agricultural services and fictional sample
data; it is not currently accepting public enquiries, orders or
marketing subscriptions.

Do not submit real names, email addresses, phone numbers, farm or
business details, messages, credentials or other personal or confidential
information through this demonstration.

## Data handled by the current prototype

- The public enquiry form and enquiry email notifications are disabled.
  No public enquiry submissions are accepted.
- Team sign-in is invite-only. If an invited team member signs in,
  Supabase Auth processes the account email and authentication data.
- Website and API providers (Vercel, Render and Supabase) may process
  technical connection and security data, such as IP addresses and
  service logs, to operate and protect their services. Exact provider
  processing regions and log-retention periods have not been
  independently verified.
- The enquiry email provider is not currently used by a public enquiry
  workflow. No enquiry notification emails are being sent.

## Retention and recovery limits

A daily seven-day database purge is configured for enquiry records, but
its first scheduled execution has not yet been verified. The current
Supabase plan does not include project backups. The prototype does not
promise deletion or recovery of provider-held logs or team-account data.
Do not use it to store real enquiries or rely on it for business records.

## Scope and limitations

This notice describes the current school-project prototype; it is not
legal advice or a claim of legal compliance. A school-project label does
not automatically exempt personal-data processing from applicable
privacy laws. Before enabling public data collection, the owner must
review the applicable obligations, provider disclosures, deletion
behavior, backup handling and contact route for that actual use.
